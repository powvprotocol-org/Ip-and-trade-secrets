# Industrial Connectivity

PoWV is designed to operate across heterogeneous industrial and logistics environments, including constrained or intermittent links.

## Connectivity model

* local sensor or industrial scale;
* edge oracle with hardware-assisted identity;
* industrial serial or IP-based link;
* secure gateway;
* terrestrial or satellite backhaul;
* verification and audit infrastructure.

## Public validation scope

The research program evaluates packet integrity, electrical-noise resistance, latency, retransmission, energy consumption, termination, isolation, and behavior across different message sizes.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Electrical parameters, field topology, production modem configuration, proprietary connectors, and customer-specific integration procedures are not published.
{% endhint %}
