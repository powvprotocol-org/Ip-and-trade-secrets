# Use Cases

## Supply Chain and Logistics

Freight verification, warehouse integrity, inventory validation, shipment custody, and rail or road logistics evidence.

## Agriculture

Commodity traceability, crop verification, warehouse receipts, and supply-chain finance.

## Recycling and Circular Economy

Reverse logistics, material recovery evidence, environmental asset verification, and ESG reporting integrity.

## Insurance

Parametric triggers, automated claims evidence, fraud reduction, and asset-condition verification.

## Government and Infrastructure

Procurement verification, operational evidence, audit transparency, and infrastructure delivery records.

## RWA Infrastructure

PoWV can act as a verification layer before physical assets are represented, financed, insured, or settled in digital systems.
